Why this draft is bigger

Playtest note from Tyler: each Roots room takes about 10 s, so the five built rooms are about a minute, and the world needs more breathing room, calm and moody spaces, and more time to teach and reinforce each mechanic. The first draft assumed 40 to 50 s per room, which was wrong by about 4 to 5 times.

  • Each spoke now has about 10 rooms in this order: calm arrival, item room, reinforcement, stakes, calm breather, a second use of the item, twist, cross-item room, calm hush, fragment room. Three of the ten are calm rooms with no enemy and no timed hazard.
  • Rooms are wider (40 to 64 tiles) so a room can hold more than one beat. A calm room adds mood. It adds time only if it holds the player (see the time estimate).
  • Each spoke has its own layout shape so the world does not feel repeated. Roots is a long corridor and a climb. Flood runs east along a top lane, down a shaft and on east below (the Flood session sets the exact positions). Molten is a zigzag that heads west, drops, runs back east and drops again to the geysers. Crystals is a tower that you climb out of and fall back down.
  • Chases reuse known rooms (R20). In Roots the calm Cathedral becomes part of the escape route. In Crystals the delete sweep erases the tower from the top as you drop back down it.

Time estimate

Two estimates, because the first draft guessed and was wrong. A uses fixed seconds per room type: built rooms 10 s (observed), calm 20 s, reinforcement 25 s, other teaching 30 to 35 s, chases 30 to 40 s. B comes from geometry: route length divided by 6 tiles per second (first-time speed; the run is 8) plus 8 s per beat, calibrated on the built rooms. A playtest replaces both.

With the intro (3 min) and the ending (2 min) added, A gives about 24:30 and B gives about 23:00, before failures (1 to 3 min). So the layout lands between 20 and 24 minutes, not a firm 25.

The gap between A and B is mostly calm rooms. A 48-tile calm room is crossed in about 8 s unless something holds the player, so a calm room adds mood but little time by itself. To make A true, each calm room needs a hold: wading through shallow water (the mascot is slower in water), a perch that triggers a sit and a music swell, a slow camera pan to a vista, or a scripted beat. Without holds, expect the B numbers. Roots is the thinnest spoke either way (3:10 to 3:10).

Known issues and decisions

  • Status 2026-11-06: Roots is built (11 rooms, tested, reviewed). Flood has 10 rooms decided by Tyler, with the board found in room 8 (about 80 percent) and rooms 1 to 7 needing none. Rooms b1 to b7, f1 (Shallows, the board room) and f2 (Hermit Cove) are built by the Flood session and drawn from the live rooms: a dip, not the old U (b1 to b5 east along the top, b6 drops through a floor hole into b7, b7 climbs east into f1). b10 Eel Grotto (80 x 32, east of f2, door in f2's east wall) is built with the Flood chase: a westward rising tide with an eel through b10, f2 and f1, ending inside f1's west door (x 253). The Flood box runs to x 470.
  • Molten is built (Molten session, 2026-11-06): m1 to m11, drawn from the live rooms, and m1 reaches the hub: a climbable shaft links m1 to the hub's south funnel, sealed with rubble until the Flood fragment is placed (m1 grew 6 rows up, to [-24,18] 48 x 26; the part of m1 inside the Roots box is solid rock). It runs west along the top (m1 to m4), drops through m4 and m5 into the middle band (m5 east to m8), then down through m9 and back west through m10 to m11 (Geyser Core). The heat-forged board is in m3 and the vine claws in m6, so the board comes about 27 percent of the way in and the claws about 55 percent. The geyser chase runs east from the m11 fragment through the vents of m10 and ends at x -92 before the seat. The bot clears it in 11 s.
  • Scope: 42 main rooms (roots 10, flood 10, molten 11, crystals 10, plus the hub), roughly 3 times the tile area of the first draft. The time estimates below are guesses until the zones are playtested; Roots has only a scripted-bot time (148 s for the whole run, a floor).
  • The Flood box grew to x 24..470 so that the late rooms fit. Tyler said the Molten box may move, which keeps the option of a one-way lane under Roots; it is not needed, because Tyler chose the Seedbed ledge (a root-jump prize) as the walk-home payoff.
  • The hub's east exit into Flood is open and ungated until decision D1 (placing each fragment opens the next hub exit) is wired with the first real Flood gate.
  • Molten has no shard nook yet (the old proposal Ms was dropped because it overlapped m5 and m6) and no return shortcut: the walk back east from the geysers is long. A one-way vent or lift needs a place in the layout.
  • The hub's top exit needs the vine claws to reach (the hub ledges are about 8 tiles up). That fits because Crystals opens after Molten.
  • The Flood shard nook (beside the shaft, needs the heat-forged board) and the Roots nook (Rs, above r5) are proposals. The map reads the live rooms, so it changes when rooms change.
