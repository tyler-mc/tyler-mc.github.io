02The launch

SOCOM: Confrontation was the first entry for the series on the PS3. It launched on October 14, 2008, as a download from PlayStation Network and at retail, with seven North African environments.

At the time of its release, its popularity took Sony by surprise. It not only took down Sony's servers for the game but for their entire online store front for a couple of days.

03Kasbah

I was responsible for the Kasbah map, one of the most popular maps on release. I focused on designing and balancing the level across all game modes. Kasbah was rated best Confrontation map in multiple community votes.

Before release, a June 2008 hands-on picked the map, then called Kazbah as a working title, as "our favorite level". The map was described as a connected series of fortresses, with towers scattered throughout that challenged snipers.
